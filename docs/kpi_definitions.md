# KPI Definitions

All currency values are Canadian dollars (CAD). The analytical period uses the complete synthetic sales range. Unless a report says otherwise, dates are interpreted as calendar dates without a time-zone conversion because the source contains dates rather than timestamps.

## KPI reference

| KPI | Definition and formula | Source and grain | Business meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total Revenue | `SUM(sales.net_revenue)` | Sales line | Money earned after line-item discounts and before returns are deducted. |
| Gross Profit | `SUM(sales.profit)` where `profit = net_revenue - cost` | Sales line | Revenue remaining after product cost. Operating expenses are not included. |
| Profit Margin | `SUM(profit) / SUM(net_revenue)` | Aggregate of sales lines | Gross profit earned per dollar of net revenue. Use the ratio of totals, not an average of row margins. |
| Orders | `COUNT(DISTINCT sales.order_id)` | Order | Number of unique customer orders. One order can contain several product lines. |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | `SUM(net_revenue) / COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)` | Order | Average net revenue generated by an order. |
| Units Sold | `SUM(sales.quantity)` | Sales line | Total physical units sold. |
| Customers | `COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)` excluding `CUST_UNKNOWN` | Customer | Number of identifiable customers who purchased. |
| Repeat Customer Rate | Customers with at least two distinct orders / customers with at least one order | Customer | Share of purchasing customers who returned for another order. |
| Return Rate | `SUM(returns.quantity_returned) / SUM(sales.quantity)` | Returned and sold units, aggregated separately | Share of sold units that were returned. This is unit-based, not return-record-based. |
| Stockout Rate | Inventory observations with `closing_stock = 0` / all inventory observations | Monthly store-product observation | How often a store-product ended a monthly observation with no stock. |
| Inventory Turnover | `SUM(sales.cost) / AVG(monthly closing inventory value)` | Category or company-period | How many times average inventory value was sold through at cost during the selected period. Inventory value is `closing_stock * product unit_cost`, summed for each month before averaging. |
| Month-over-Month Growth | `(current month revenue - previous month revenue) / previous month revenue` | Calendar month | Percentage change in revenue from the previous complete month. The first month has no comparison. |
| Average Discount | `AVG(sales.discount_pct)` | Sales line | Average discount applied to a sales line. This is not weighted by revenue or units unless the report explicitly says so. |

## Calculation details

### Total Revenue

```text
gross_revenue = quantity * unit_price
net_revenue   = gross_revenue * (1 - discount_pct)
total revenue = sum(net_revenue)
```

Revenue is calculated after discounts. Taxes, shipping, refunds, and operating expenses are outside this project’s scope.

### Gross Profit and Profit Margin

```text
cost          = quantity * unit_cost
profit        = net_revenue - cost
profit margin = total profit / total revenue
```

Profit is gross product profit, not accounting net income. A category can have high revenue but a weak margin when its costs or discounts are high.

### Orders and Average Order Value

An order may contain one to four product lines. Count distinct `order_id` values so multi-line orders are not counted more than once.

```text
AOV = total revenue / distinct orders
```

### Return Rate

Sales and returns must be aggregated separately before the numerator and denominator are combined. Joining raw sales lines directly to returns and then summing both sides can duplicate sales values.

```text
return rate = returned units / sold units
```

The standard Power BI measure is an operational period rate: return events are filtered by return date, while sales are filtered by order date. A true purchase-cohort return rate would require assigning every return to the original order cohort.

### Stockout Rate

```text
stockout rate = zero-closing-stock observations / inventory observations
```

Inventory is monthly at `date + store_id + product_id` grain. This rate therefore measures monthly snapshot frequency, not the percentage of hours or days an item was unavailable.

### Inventory Turnover

```text
monthly inventory value = sum(closing_stock * unit_cost) for each month
average inventory value = average(monthly inventory value)
inventory turnover      = cost of goods sold / average inventory value
```

Turnover depends on the selected period. Always show the date range beside this KPI.

### Month-over-Month Growth

```text
MoM growth = (current month revenue - prior month revenue) / prior month revenue
```

Use complete, consecutive calendar months. Return blank when the prior month is unavailable or its revenue is zero.

## Decision guardrails

- Review profit margin beside revenue so discount-led growth is not mistaken for healthy growth.
- Review return rate beside product/category growth so quality issues remain visible.
- Review stockout rate beside units sold; low sales can reflect low demand or unavailable inventory.
- Do not treat discount-band differences as causal evidence. The project shows association only.
- Display the analysis period and currency on every dashboard page.
